Before we arrive

Before We Arrive: First Moves in a Pickerington Home

Work through these in order, and keep your hands dry near anything electrical.

  1. Close the main

    Find the shutoff where the water line enters the house, often by the meter or in the basement, and turn it clockwise until it stops.

  2. Cut power near water

    If the leak is at an electric water heater or near outlets, switch off that breaker. Do not stand in water to reach the panel.

  3. Clear the floor

    Move rugs, boxes and electronics out of the wet zone, then set a bucket under any drip.

  4. Call dispatch

    Ring our 24/7 line and give us the street, the ZIP and what you have already shut off.
