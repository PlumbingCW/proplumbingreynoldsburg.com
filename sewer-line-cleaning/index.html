Sewer line cleaning is the removal of roots, grease, paper and debris from the main drain that runs from your house to the public sewer, using a cable machine or a water jet. In Reynoldsburg, OH it is the fix for a backup that hits several fixtures at once.

Sewage in the house is urgent, and we dispatch 24/7. Until we arrive, stop using water, close the toilet lids, and keep people off any wet floor.

Why the basement drain gives out first

Gravity does the work in a sewer line, so wastewater from every fixture ends up at the lowest opening when the main is restricted. In many homes that is a basement floor drain, a laundry tub or a lowest-level shower. If those bubble or fill while upstairs fixtures run, the problem is probably the main, not a single trap.

A single slow sink is a different story, and a branch line cable usually handles it. Our drain cleaning service covers those smaller lines.

Side by side

Cable machine or water jet for the main

Both clear a main line. We pick based on what is in it.

Cable machine

  • Cuts through roots with a blade head
  • Reaches long runs and bends well
  • Opens a channel but may leave a ring of buildup
  • Quick for a first-time blockage

Water jet

  • Cleans the full pipe wall around the circle
  • Best for grease and repeat clogs
  • Needs sound pipe to be safe
  • Water pressure from hydro jetting suits heavy buildup
Act first

When sewage is on the floor or in the tub

If wastewater is rising in a tub or flooding a basement drain, the main is likely blocked and more water will make it worse. We dispatch around the clock for this, and the first job is to open the line so the house can drain again.

  • No flushing, laundry or dishwasher until the line is open
  • Close doors to protect rooms that are still dry
  • Wipe or lift small items off the affected floor
  • Skip chemical openers, which splash back and harm pipe and plumbers
  • Let us know if the cleanout cap outside is under snow or soil
How it works

How we clean a main line

We follow a routine that avoids pushing the problem further down the pipe.

1

Open the cleanout

We unscrew the cap slowly, since wastewater can sit behind it under pressure.

2

Cable first

A machine with the right cutter head bores through the blockage and reports what it feels, such as roots or a sudden drop.

3

Flush and test

We run water and watch the flow at several fixtures to confirm the line drains steadily.

4

Look at the pipe

If roots, scale or a belly turned up, a camera look at the line shows the cause.

5

Plan the next step

Clean and watch, schedule recurring cleaning, or move to repair, depending on what the pipe shows.

Sewer drain and main sewer cleaning, plainly defined

People use several names for the same work: sewer drain cleaning, main sewer cleaning, rodding the sewer. What they share is that the equipment goes in through a cleanout and works toward the street. The difference lies in what is in the pipe and which tool you choose.

A first-time blockage from paper or a toy can clear in one pass. A line packed with roots or grease needs patience and often a follow-up visit.

Reynoldsburg, OH

Reynoldsburg sewer connections and your cleanout

The public main downstream of your property may be a utility matter, since Reynoldsburg buys sewer service from Columbus as a contract community. Cleaning your private line only helps if the blockage is on your side, so ask the city who maintains the pipe past your lot line.

Finding your cleanout before a backup saves time. Look for a threaded cap on a pipe low on a wall, outside or in the basement. If a blockage keeps returning, ask us about repairing the damaged section rather than paying for the same cleaning.

Checklist

What not to flush or pour if you want the line to stay clear

Habits cause more main line clogs than bad luck.

  • Wipes labeled flushable, which do not break apart in pipe
  • Cooking grease and oil poured while still warm
  • Paper towels, feminine products and cotton swabs
  • Coffee grounds and eggshell in kitchen drains
  • Large wads of hair from shower and tub drains
