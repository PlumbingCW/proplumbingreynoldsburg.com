Reynoldsburg: ZIPs 43068 and 43069

Reynoldsburg is our home ground. In ZIP 43068, we handle drain cleaning for kitchen and main lines, along with toilet repair and fixture work in homes near E Main Street and City Hall. In ZIP 43069, water heater repair and leak detection come up most often.

The city is mostly in Franklin County, with small parts in Fairfield and Licking counties, so confirm which office handles your address. The main roads are E Main Street, E Broad Street and Brice Road, and I-70 runs along the southern edge. A cross street helps us route a truck fast.

Pickerington

Pickerington plumbing calls reach us from Olde Pickerington Village and the neighborhoods around State Route 256. The city runs its own well system, and a 2016 report described filtration for iron and manganese. Homes there may see different water behavior than homes on Columbus water, so tell the plumber if you have filters or a softener already.

Gahanna

Gahanna is a Columbus water and sewer contract community like Reynoldsburg. Homes near Big Walnut Creek and its tributaries can have damp basements after storms, so a sump pump check is often part of the visit. Call us with your cross street, because State Route 317 and I-270 shape the fastest route.

Whitehall

Whitehall grew up mostly after its 1947 incorporation, which means many homes carry original supply lines and older drains. We see clogged lateral lines and corroded fixtures there. Columbus also lists Whitehall as a water and sewer contract community, so ask your city where the public main begins.

Why the boundary question matters

Reynoldsburg shares edges with several communities, and a few homes sit in Fairfield or Licking county even though the mailing address says Reynoldsburg. That can change which office handles inspections or which utility owns a line. Ask your water utility or the Franklin County Public Health plumbing office when you are unsure, and tell your plumber what you learn.

Water and sewer service also differ by town. Columbus lists Reynoldsburg, Gahanna and Whitehall as Water and Sewer Contract Communities, while Pickerington runs its own water system. When a leak sits near the street, knowing who owns that pipe can save a long phone chase, and our water line repair page explains the usual split.

Nearby places we also reach

Blacklick Estates sits southwest of Reynoldsburg and Etna Township sits to the east. Both are within reach of a normal dispatch, and Columbus itself is next door. Call with your address and we will confirm that a plumber can come.
