Before we arrive

Before We Arrive: Whitehall Water Emergency Checklist

Do the first two within a minute. The rest can follow.

  1. Stop the flow

    Turn the main valve clockwise where the line enters your house. Old valves can be stiff, so use both hands and steady pressure.

  2. Breaker off

    Flip circuits serving wet rooms. If water is near the panel, do not touch it and call us.

  3. Empty the wet zone

    Move furniture, rugs and anything electrical away from the leak, then put towels around the edge.

  4. Reach dispatch

    Call the 24/7 number with your address and the room where water appears. We will confirm what to leave alone.
